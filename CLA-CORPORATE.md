**Version 1.0**

This agreement is for a **company or other legal entity** whose employees or agents
contribute to Wallgraph ("the Project"), owned and maintained by Jeffrey Ernst ("the
Maintainer").

It exists because in most jurisdictions, code written by an employee in the course of
their employment is owned by the employer, not the employee. An individual CLA signed by
such an employee may therefore grant rights they do not personally hold. This agreement
closes that gap.

Wallgraph is released publicly under the GNU AGPL v3, and the Maintainer additionally
offers it under separate commercial terms. Section 2 is what makes that dual-licensing
possible.

in and to its Contributions.
